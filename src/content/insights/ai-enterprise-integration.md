### Phase 2: Data Readiness Assessment

AI is only as good as its data foundation:

- Audit data quality, completeness, and accessibility
- Establish data pipelines with proper governance
- Ensure compliance with privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA)

### Phase 4: Production Hardening

The gap between prototype and production is where most initiatives fail:

- Implement comprehensive monitoring and observability
- Build feedback loops for continuous model improvement
- Design graceful degradation paths when models underperform
